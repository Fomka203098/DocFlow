# DocFlow

DocFlow is a desktop-first workspace for checking applications. Keep upload, history and rules in the top navigation. On the report screen put the findings and download actions next to the original document.

White and cool gray surfaces with graphite text keep the work area clear. The introduction has a pale blue background that separates it from the workspace. Cobalt identifies primary actions, focus and the logo. Green, yellow and red distinguish extracted fields, manual review and errors. Status labels accompany color. The logo pairs two overlapping page outlines with a check mark.

Use Rubik for the interface, self-hosted with Latin and Cyrillic, and a serif for extracted document text. Use consistent button heights and group actions by task. Keep form errors next to the relevant input and preserve keyboard focus during polling.

The opening demonstration animates two passes over a photorealistic stack of four sample office forms, then stops. The transparent raster asset was created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The progress indicator moves only during an actual check. Report entry and button feedback are short. Respect the user's reduced-motion preference.

References: [Linear navigation and density](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh), [Readwise Reader document and annotation layout](https://readwise.io/read), [Emil Kowalski's animation examples](https://animations.dev/).
